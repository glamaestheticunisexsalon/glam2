Understanding a chemical peel.

A chemical peel uses a selected formulation in a professional setting. A consultation gives you a chance to understand the approach, suitability and care involved before making a decision.

What is a chemical peel?

Peels vary by formulation and strength. A qualified professional can explain the type being considered and whether it is appropriate for your skin and circumstances.

What should a consultation cover?

  • Your skin history, current products and relevant sensitivities.
  • Expected preparation and any products to pause, if advised.
  • Possible reactions, risks and alternatives.
  • How to care for your skin afterwards and when to seek advice.
  • Whether follow-up or repeat sessions may be suitable.

How do I prepare?

Follow the specific instructions from your qualified professional. Do not start or stop active skin-care products without asking them, and contact the provider if your skin changes before your appointment.
