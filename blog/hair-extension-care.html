A considered guide to hair extensions.

Hair extensions can add length, volume or styling options. The best starting point is a conversation with an experienced stylist about your hair, routine and expectations.

What happens at a consultation?

Your stylist may discuss your desired result, hair condition, colour match, attachment options, comfort, maintenance schedule and home care. Ask how each option may interact with your own hair and what maintenance commitments to expect.

How can I care for extensions?

  • Follow the product and brushing advice provided by your stylist.
  • Detangle gently, working from the ends upward while supporting the hair.
  • Use only products recommended for your extension type.
  • Attend maintenance appointments at the interval suggested for your method.
  • Speak to your stylist if you notice discomfort, tangling or a change in your natural hair.

Is one method right for everyone?

No. Hair type, condition, lifestyle and the extension method all matter. A consultation helps you compare options; ask a qualified hair professional for advice specific to you.
